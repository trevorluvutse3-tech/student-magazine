University lectures end 28-day strike as fresh tension emerge over CBA deal

5 November 2026|National News

Learning activities in public universities across Kenya faced major disruption following a nationwide lectures strike that began on 2 October 2026

The industrial action was led by university staff union, including the Universities Academic Staff Union(UASU), over disagremment surrounding the 2025-2029 Collective Bargaining Agreement(CBA)

On 30 October 2026, the union called off the 28-day strike after reaching a ksh 20 billion agreement with the government. The deal provides for salary increases and improved allowances with funding to be released in faces

However, on 5 November, UASU warned that another strike could happen if the government fails to honor the funding committment and address outstanding concerns

The prolonged indusrial action disrupted academic calenders, affecting learning activities and raised concerns among students about delaied examination and completion of programmes

The lectures strike highlighted ongoing disagreement over university staff wellfare, funding and collective bargaining agreements.
